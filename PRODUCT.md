# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Oncology patients (and their caregivers) managing treatment while experiencing "chemo brain" cognitive impairment. They arrive exhausted, often on a laptop between appointments, looking for relief from medical admin. Secondary audience: clinicians and hackathon/demo judges evaluating the product.

## Product Purpose

Rivertide is a wellness OS for oncology: agentic voice dictation (`fn`), a command mode with integrations (`ctrl + fn`), an encrypted memory vault, and cognitive-load-adaptive UI. The site's job is to make the product feel calm, capable, and trustworthy enough to sign up for the waitlist.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, tactile. "Premium consumer hardware" feel in software: physical keycaps, textured buttons, soft light. Reassuring, never clinical-cold or startup-loud. Reference points: wisprflow.ai (keycap chips, soft raised cards) and cluely.com (textured/shiny buttons, floating glass nav) — recolored to Rivertide's deep teals, never their palettes wholesale.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS gradients (purple mesh, glassmorphism on everything)
- Hospital-portal sterility (Epic/MyChart grey)
- Flat untextured pill buttons with no material feel

## Design Principles

1. **Physical, not flat** — interactive elements (buttons, keys) read as objects with depth, shine, and a press.
2. **Calm confidence** — deep teal carries authority; lavender and warm off-white keep it human.
3. **Voice is the hero** — keyboard shortcuts (`fn`, `ctrl + fn`) are brand icons; render them as real keys everywhere.
4. **Preserve identity** — existing palette, type (Manrope + JetBrains Mono), and IA stay; materials and craft improve.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Users may have cognitive fatigue and reduced motor precision: WCAG AA contrast minimum, generous hit targets, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored for all looping/auto animations.
